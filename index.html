Sequence your own finals

One deadline per line: name, YYYY-MM-DD, then H for a heavy one and X for an exam that can't move. Optional daily tasks: YYYY-MM-DD, name, task. Approach control tries every landing order and picks the one that moves the least work earlier while keeping 48 h between landings and 72 h behind a heavy.

How the scope works

Range rings are daysEach deadline is an arrival flying straight in. Its distance from the runway is the days until it lands (the day you submit it). The scope zooms from 28 to 7 days as finals close in.
Separation: 48 hNo two landings within 48 hours. Behind a H/ heavy one, 72 hours, the way controllers leave extra space behind a heavy aircraft for its wake turbulence. Here the wake is how tired you are.
Conflict alertThe official due dates had the OS project and the stats report 24 hours apart. Gemini, playing approach control, landed the report 3 days early (Dec 7, due Dec 10) ahead of the OS project (Dec 9).
FAA colour codegreen: not started yet (unowned) · white: in progress (yours) · yellow: today · blue: the days already flown · red: conflict. Black background, dim gray rings: FAA JO 7210.3 §3-9-1.
Runway 18The last landing is Friday Dec 18, so everything lands on runway 18. Your own plan gets the runway of its last date.
